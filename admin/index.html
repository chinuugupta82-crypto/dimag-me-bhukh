    How this admin works

    1. Pick a section on the left and change the words. The preview shows your changes.
    2. Press Publish to website. Your website updates by itself in about a minute.

    Writing a blog post

    Press ✍️ New blog post, write it, set Status to Published and press Publish to website. It then shows in the Blog section of your home page and gets its own page. Posts left on Draft stay private.

    The order of the sections on your website follows Menu & section order (under Settings): move an entry up or down and its section moves too.

    The first time, press Publish and fill in the connection details (see Connection settings). After that it is one click.

    Photos

    Click Choose file under a photo to replace it. Photos are made smaller automatically. Add a short description for every photo.

    Formatting words

    A blank line starts a new paragraph. *italic* gives italic, **bold** gives bold, and [text](https://address) makes a link.

    Cards and lists

    Open a card to edit it. Use ↑ ↓ to move it, Copy to duplicate it and Delete to remove it. + Add makes a new one.

    Good to know

    • Your draft is kept in this browser. Use the same computer and browser next time, or download often.
    • Nothing goes live until you press Publish. Only someone with the secret key can publish.
    • More → Older versions brings back an earlier version. More → Download ZIP saves a backup on your computer.

# How to edit the website (for Akanksha)

1. Open your website address followed by `/admin/`, for example `yourwebsite.com/admin/`.
2. Choose a section on the left and change the words. Press **Preview** to see your website next to the form.
3. When you are happy, press **Publish to website**. Wait about one minute, then refresh your website.

That is all. Nothing to download and nothing to upload.

## Changing things
- **Words:** click in the box and type. A blank line starts a new paragraph. `*italic*`, `**bold**` and `[text](https://address)` work too.
- **A photo:** under the photo choose a new file. It is made smaller automatically. Add a short description for each photo.
- **Add something new:** open the section and press **+ Add** (a new myth, story, step, topic or card).
- **Move, copy or delete a card:** use ↑ ↓, **Copy** and **Delete** on the card.
- **Links and email:** Site settings. Paste your Instagram link there and the Instagram icon appears.
- **The words Google shows:** Site settings, "Page title" and "Description".


## Latest Updates (your YouTube videos)
Near the top of the home page there is a **Latest Updates** band that plays your **newest YouTube video**. It changes by itself every time you upload a new video. You do nothing.
- To add a few videos of your own choice under it: admin → **🎬 Latest Updates (YouTube)** → **+ Add video**, type a title and paste the YouTube link.
- To hide the band: set **Show this section** to **No** and Publish.
- The video only plays on the live website (the admin preview shows a placeholder).

## Writing a post (blog)
Quickest way: press **✍️ New blog post** at the top of the admin, write, set Status to **Published**, press **Publish to website**. Your post then shows in the **Blog** section of the home page.

Step by step:
1. In the admin, open **📝 Blog (write here)** (it is first in the list) and press **+ Add post** (or **✍️ New blog post** at the top).
2. Write the **title**. The page name (web address) is made for you.
3. Add a **short summary**, a **category** (for example "Food and feeding") and, if you like, a **cover photo**.
4. Write the post in the big box. Use the buttons above it: **B** bold, **I** italic, **Heading**, **• List**, **Quote**, **Link**, and **📷 Photo** to put a photo inside the post. A blank line starts a new paragraph.
5. Set **Status** to **Published** when it is ready. Posts marked **Draft** stay private.
6. Press **Publish to website**. In about a minute the post has its own page, appears in the **Blog** section on the home page (the newest three; a button leads to all of them) and in the all-blog-posts page, and is added to the search-engine list.
   If you press Publish while a post you just wrote is still on **Draft**, the admin reminds you that visitors will not see it yet.

- **Change a post later:** open it, edit, and Publish again.
- **Hide a post:** set it back to **Draft** and Publish. Its page is removed.
- **Delete a post:** press **Delete** on the post and Publish.
- **Two starter drafts** are already there (about rice, and what parents feed chicks). Read them, change anything you like, and publish them if you are happy.
- The **Blog** section of the home page (right under Latest Updates) and the **Blog** link at the top and in the menu are always there. Until the first post is published, the section shows a short "coming soon" note with your Facebook and YouTube buttons. You can change that note under **Blog (write here) → Blog section settings**.
- Fill in **Website address** in Site settings after launch so search engines and the post feed work.

## Changing the order of the sections
Open **Settings → ☰ Menu & section order** and use ↑ ↓ on an entry. The pop-up menu **and** the sections on the website both follow this order (Home always stays first), and the background colours keep alternating by themselves. Press **Publish** when you like it.

The recommended order is: Home, Latest Updates, Blog, About, My 3-Year Journey, What I Have Observed, Knowledge Hub, How to Invite Sparrows, Sparrow-Friendly Home, In Depth, Babies & Nesting, Myths vs Facts, Rescue & Help, Stories, Social media, Share Your Idea, Contact.

## Mistakes and backups
- Nothing changes on the website until you press **Publish**. Your draft is kept in this browser.
- **More → Discard my draft** throws away unpublished changes.
- **More → Older versions** brings back an earlier published version. Load it, check it, then Publish.
- **More → Download ZIP** saves a copy of everything on your computer.

## If something goes wrong
- **"Key not accepted" or "no permission":** the secret key has expired. Ask the person who set up the website for a new connection code (More → Connection settings).
- **"Could not reach GitHub":** check your internet and try again.
- **Website does not show the change:** wait a minute and press Ctrl + F5 to refresh fully.
